# Research Notes

## Challenges

- High false positives in rule-based systems
- Normal traffic being flagged as malicious
- Lack of temporal awareness

## Solutions Applied

- Introduced packet rate (time-based detection)
- Multi-signal validation
- Trust scoring layer
- Behavioral drift tracking

## Observations

- CDN and cloud traffic often appear suspicious
- Frequency-based detection is unreliable
- Risk must be contextual, not absolute
